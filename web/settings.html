Calendar

Shows your next events on the TV. It needs the calendar's link: in Google Calendar the "Secret address in iCal format", in iCloud the link of a public calendar, in Synology Calendar the published link.

    Smart home

    Lights, plugs, scenes and sensors from Home Assistant, as tiles in the app row.

      The token is made in Home Assistant: your profile, Security, Long-lived access tokens. Locks and alarms are left out on purpose.

      Radio

      Stations appear as tiles in the app row and play while the photos carry on. The remote's play button pauses.

        Screen

        Up and Down on the remote switch between these three at any time.

        The background stands still and photos stop moving until the Pi has cooled down.

        Night

        Between these times the TV shows only a dim clock on black. Any button brings the dashboard back for a minute.

        Apps

        These appear in the app row on the TV, in this order.

          A program that needs its own command or options can be added in ~/.config/pidash/config.toml on the Pi. It then shows up here to reorder or remove.

          Lock

          With a PIN, settings can be looked at but not changed without it.

          Updates

          Installed at night, between three and five, and never while an app is open.

          Backup

          One file with everything set up here, to move to another Pi or a new memory card. It contains the passwords and links stored on the Pi, so keep it to yourself.

          Files can only be saved and loaded on a computer or phone: open this TV's address followed by /settings there.
