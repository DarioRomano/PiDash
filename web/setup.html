Welcome to PiDash

Three questions, and the TV is yours. ▲ ▼ move, OK chooses, Back goes back.

Which language?

Where is this TV?

For the weather, the rain radar and the times of sunrise and sunset. A town is enough.

Where are your photos?

They become the picture on the TV. The page after this one asks for what it takes to reach them: an address and an account.

That is all

  • OK on the dashboard opens the app row. Settings is one of its tiles: apps, radio and everything else are added there.
  • ▲ on the dashboard is about the photo that is showing; ▼ changes how much is shown beside it.
  • Inside an app, holding Back, or pressing it three times quickly, returns to the dashboard.
